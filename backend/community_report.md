## Summary
The Amazon Distribution Center in Clear Brook has a roof covering 23.9 acres. This change increases the volume of runoff during storms compared with previous land cover.

## What changed
The roof is estimated to add 486736 gallons of runoff in a 1 inch storm, 973472 gallons in a 2 inch storm, and 1460208 gallons in a 3 inch storm.

## Where water may travel
Modeled overland flow paths extend 37.85 km through a screening corridor buffered at 40 m and covering 218.37 hectares.

## Features in the screening corridor
There are 102 buildings, 164 road segments totaling 15.59 km, 0 schools, and 41 wetland features covering 15.62 hectares inside the modeled screening corridor.

## Important limitations
Storm drains, culverts, detention ponds, and underground infrastructure are not modeled. This is a screening estimate, not an engineering flood study.
